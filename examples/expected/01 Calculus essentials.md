## 1.1 The derivative

For a function $f$, the derivative at $x$ is

 

$$
f^{\prime}(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}.
$$

 

For example, if $f(x) = x^{2}$ then $f'(x) = 2x$, and the Gaussian integral is

 

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x=\sqrt{\pi}.
$$
